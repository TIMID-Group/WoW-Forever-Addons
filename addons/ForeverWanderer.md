## 💡 What it is

The Legacy Challenges' Explore achievements want every corner of a zone, and the last few places are always the hardest to find. Forever Wanderer adds a **Start journey** button to each Explore card and **Explore this zone** to the world map. One click makes the shortest journey from where you stand through every place this character has not discovered yet, and it moves on by itself as you discover them.

## ✨ Key features

- **One click per zone or continent.** Every zone's Explore card in the Legacy Challenges window gets a Start journey button, and so do Explore Kalimdor and Explore Eastern Kingdoms.
- **Explore this zone on the world map.** The same journey for whichever zone you are looking at.
- **The shortest order.** The journey starts where you stand, nearest first, then shortened, and leaves out every place you have explored already.
- **It moves on by itself.** Discover a place, even by passing its edge on the way somewhere else, and it is dropped from the journey within a second.
- **Nothing missed.** A place you reached without discovering it is named at the end, and Try again aims deeper inside.
- **Every place is known.** 549 places in 43 zones, from WoW Forever's own game data, including Riverglades, Mount Hyjal and Shen'dralas.
- **Picks up where you left off.** After a reload or a relog the journey comes back by itself.

## 🎯 Who it's for

- **You chase the Explore achievements** and want the last few places found without a map in another window.
- **You are levelling a new character** and want to fill in the map on the way.
- **You like to wander,** but would rather not walk past the one subzone you still need.

## 🧭 How it works

1. **Get access and install** with WowUp, as described in [Get it](#-get-it).
2. **Open the Legacy Challenges window,** go to Adventure, then Kalimdor or Eastern Kingdoms, and press **Start journey** on an Explore card. Or open the world map and press **Explore this zone**.
3. **Follow the waypoints.** Each place drops off the journey as you discover it.
4. **Press the button again** to stop at any time.

## 🔗 Works with

Wanderer needs nothing else: on its own, the game's map pin goes on the next place and moves on as you discover places.

- **ShortestPathForever.** One journey through every place, with flights and boats, travel times, and walking distances for the order.
- **TomTom.** Every place of the journey as a waypoint, each removed as you discover it.
- **Every TIMID addon.** With two or more installed, their minimap buttons fold into one TIMID button.

## ❓ FAQ

### Does it need other addons?

No. ShortestPathForever or TomTom make the journey easier to follow, but the game's own map pin works too.

### Does it open a window?

No. It has no window of its own: just the buttons on the Explore cards and the world map, and its settings.

### Will it slow my game?

A journey's order is worked out a little each frame, so the game never stutters, and a discovery is checked in a fraction of a millisecond.

### Is my data private?

Yes. Your settings stay on your computer. A problem report holds nothing about your character, realm, guild, gold, account or where you stand, and it leaves the game only when you copy and paste it yourself.

### How do I report a problem?

Use Report a problem on its settings pages, or type `/wanderer report`, copy the link and paste it into your browser. The form opens already filled in.
