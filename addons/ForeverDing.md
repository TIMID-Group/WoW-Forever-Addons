## 💡 What it is

A full quest log raises one question before every trip to town. Will handing these in level me up? Forever Ding adds up the XP of every quest you are ready to hand in, in the zone you are in and across the world, and shows the level that takes you to. "Ding" is what players say when they level up.

## ✨ Key features

- **The sum, done for you.** Ding adds up the XP each finished quest would give you now, the same figure the game shows on the quest's reward.
- **Here and everywhere.** Every sum comes twice, once for the quests you hand in in your current zone and once for all of them.
- **How close you are.** When the quests fall short, Ding says how much XP you would still need. When they reach a level, it says how far into the next one they take you.
- **At a glance on the minimap.** Point at the button for both sums, such as "Hand in the 3 here first: 38 to 38.6".
- **A small window with every ready quest.** See both sums with a bar showing your XP and what the quests add, and every ready quest with its zone and XP, sorted the way you like.
- **One chat line, if you want it.** Turn it on and Ding says once, at the moment your ready quests first add up to a new level, that you can ding.
- **Plan ahead.** Count the quests you have not finished yet, to see whether finishing your log would level you.
- **Useful at the top level too.** With no XP left to gain, Ding shows the gold your quests pay instead.

## 🎯 Who it's for

- **You have a full quest log** and wonder if handing it all in levels you before the next dungeon.
- **You're heading to town** and want to know whether the quests here are enough by themselves.
- **You like to save your hand-ins** and ding in one go, and want to know when you have enough.
- **You're close to a new level** and want to know exactly how much XP is still missing.

## 🧭 How it works

1. **Get access and install** with WowUp, as described in [Get it](#-get-it).
2. **Finish some quests.** Ding reads your quest log on its own.
3. **Point at the gold burst on the minimap** for the sums, or type `/ding` for the window with every ready quest.
4. **Hand in and ding.**

## 🔗 Works with

Ding needs nothing else.

- **QuestieDB.** Where each quest is handed in, so "in your zone" counts the quests you can hand in where you stand. Without it, Ding goes by the quest log's zone headings.
- **Questie.** A second source for a quest's XP while the game has not loaded the reward yet.
- **Every TIMID addon.** With two or more installed, their minimap buttons fold into one TIMID button, and Ding's row gives the short version, such as "Ding! 7 ready (+1.4 lv)".

## ❓ FAQ

### Does it need other addons?

No. QuestieDB and Questie make "in your zone" and the XP figures more complete when you have them.

### Will it slow my game?

Ding does one small sum over your quest log, the same figures the game already shows on each quest's reward.

### Does it ever act on its own?

No. It never opens by itself and says nothing in chat unless you turn on its one chat line. It never hands in a quest for you.

### Is my data private?

Yes. Your settings stay on your computer. A problem report holds nothing about your character, realm, guild, gold or account, and it leaves the game only when you copy and paste it yourself.

### How do I report a problem?

Click the small red exclamation beside the gear on the Ding window, copy the link and paste it into your browser. The form opens already filled in. You can also use the [report form](https://github.com/TIMID-Group/WoW-Forever-Addons/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) on the hub, open to everyone, invited or not.
