## 💡 What it is

Questing fills your bags with items whose quests are long finished, and it is hard to tell them apart from the ones you still need. Forever Quest Leftovers looks through your bags and bank, lists what no quest needs any more, says why, and says what to do with each item. Dungeon keys, attunements and boss summons get their own list as worth keeping, so a dungeon key doesn't end up at a vendor by mistake. It only advises, and the choice stays with you.

## ✨ Key features

- **Leftovers found for you.** Your bags and bank are checked for items left after a quest was handed in or abandoned, items that started a quest you have finished, and quest junk.
- **Certain or probable, with a reason.** Each item is filed as certainly or probably left over, with a short tag saying why.
- **What to do with each one.** Hover an item for plain advice on what to do with it.
- **Worth keeping.** Dungeon keys, attunements, boss summons and ways into a dungeon are listed apart in green, each with one line on what it is for, from a table of 44 items checked one by one.
- **Marks in your bags.** Left-over items get an orange outline in the game's bag windows and the bank, and items worth keeping get a green one.
- **Tooltips that explain.** A quest item that is still needed names the quest that needs it.
- **All your characters.** A card for each of your characters shows their leftovers too, as of the last time you played them.
- **Your call, always.** Right-click an item to keep it and stop flagging it. Nothing is ever sold or destroyed for you.

## 🎯 Who it's for

- **Your bags are full after a questing session** and you want to know what is safe to vendor.
- **Your bank is full of things from months ago** and you no longer remember which quest each one was for.
- **You've found an odd key or a strange tome** and wonder whether a dungeon or raid still needs it.
- **You play several characters** and want one place to see what each of them is carrying for no reason.

## 🧭 How it works

1. **Get access and install** with WowUp, as described in [Get it](#-get-it).
2. **Play as normal.** Quest Leftovers remembers the items of the quests in your log, so it knows what a hand-in leaves behind.
3. **Open the list** with `/leftovers` or the bag on the minimap. It groups your items by character, bags and bank.
4. **Act on the advice.** Hover an item for what to do, then sell, bank or delete it yourself. Visit your bank once so its contents are on the list too.

## 🔗 Works with

Quest Leftovers needs nothing else.

- **Questie.** Tells it which quest an item belongs to, including quests from before you installed Quest Leftovers.
- **Forever Dungeon Journal** (a separate addon). Tells it the boss summons and dungeon quest rewards of Forever's own dungeons, so they are kept as worth keeping.
- **Every TIMID addon.** With two or more installed, their minimap buttons fold into one TIMID button, and Quest Leftovers' row shows how many items look left over.

## ❓ FAQ

### Does it need other addons?

No. It reads your quest log and your bags on its own. Questie and Forever Dungeon Journal make its answers more complete when you have them.

### Will it slow my game?

It looks through your bags when they change. During an auction house scan it waits for the game to settle, then looks once.

### Does it ever act on its own?

No. It never sells, destroys or moves anything, and the list never opens by itself. A chat line when a quest leaves items behind is available, and it is off until you turn it on.

### Is my data private?

Yes. Your settings, kept items and remembered quests stay on your computer. A problem report holds nothing about your character, realm, guild, gold or account, and it leaves the game only when you copy and paste it yourself.

### How do I report a problem?

Click the small red exclamation beside the gear on the list window, copy the link and paste it into your browser. The form opens already filled in. You can also use the [report form](https://github.com/TIMID-Group/WoW-Forever-Addons/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) on the hub, open to everyone, invited or not.
