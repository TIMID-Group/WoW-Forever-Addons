## 💡 What it is

Fishing in WoW Forever raises the same questions every time. Can I cast here yet? Will fish still get away? Does the fish I want bite at this time of day? Forever Fishing Chart answers them on the game's own world map, with a pin for every charted water, a fish finder to search by fish or zone, and a log of what you have really caught.

## ✨ Key features

- **Every water on the map.** Each charted water gets a pin showing fresh, salt or river water, with darker blue for harder water and a red rim where you cannot cast yet.
- **Your skill at a glance.** On continent maps, each zone shows the skill you need to cast and the skill at which nothing gets away, coloured for your own skill.
- **Catches by time of day.** Point at a pin for its catch table, with the current six-hour window of realm time lit, so you see what bites now and what is at its best.
- **A fish finder.** Search every fish, see Biting Now at a glance, and pin a fish to light up every water where it bites.
- **Zones, easiest first.** A list of every charted zone with the skill it needs, and Where I Am to open the water you are standing at.
- **Your own catch log.** Every catch is counted by zone, sub-zone and time of day, so you learn what really bites on your realm.
- **Honest data.** Every entry says whether it was reported from the Forever client, comes from Classic-era data or is still unconfirmed, and words like "common" or "rare" stand in where nobody measured a figure.
- **Waypoints in one click.** Click a pin to set a waypoint to that water.

## 🎯 Who it's for

- **You're levelling Fishing.** See which waters you can fish without catches getting away, zone by zone.
- **You need one particular fish** for a recipe. Pin it and every water where it bites lights up on the map.
- **You wonder if now is a good time.** Biting Now and the lit time window tell you what bites at this hour of realm time.
- **You like your own numbers.** The catch log shows what you have actually pulled out of each water, by time of day.

## 🧭 How it works

1. **Get access and install** with WowUp, as described in [Get it](#-get-it).
2. **Open the world map.** Pins appear on every charted water. Point at one for its catches.
3. **Open the fish finder** with `/ffc`, the fishing icon on the minimap, or the Fishing Chart button on the world map. Search a fish and pin it.
4. **Go fishing.** Your catches are counted as you loot them, and My Log fills up.

## 🔗 Works with

Fishing Chart needs nothing else.

- **TomTom.** Clicking a pin, or pressing Set Waypoint, sets a TomTom arrow in place of the game's map pin.
- **Every TIMID addon.** With two or more installed, their minimap buttons fold into one TIMID button, and Fishing Chart's row shows your zone and the skill its water needs.

## ❓ FAQ

### Does it need other addons?

No. TomTom is optional and only changes how waypoints are shown.

### Will it slow my game?

The chart is data the addon carries with it. It draws pins when you open the world map and counts a catch when you loot one.

### Does it ever act on its own?

No. It prints nothing in chat and opens nothing unless you ask. The catch log records your catches, and you can pause it or turn it off in the settings.

### Is my data private?

Yes. Your catch log, settings and pin marks stay on your computer. A problem report holds nothing about your character, realm, guild, gold or account, and it leaves the game only when you copy and paste it yourself.

### How do I report a problem?

Click the small red exclamation beside the gear on the fish finder, copy the link and paste it into your browser. The form opens already filled in. You can also use the [report form](https://github.com/TIMID-Group/WoW-Forever-Addons/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) on the hub, open to everyone, invited or not.
