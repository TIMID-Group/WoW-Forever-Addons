## 💡 What it is

Levelling a profession means a guide in one window, a calculator in another, and a lot of trips to trainers and vendors. Forever Apprentice puts the route from the WoW Forever profession guides in the game: every step from your skill to the one you want, what it all costs, where to go, and one click to send the crafts to Forever Workbench and the shopping to your shopping list.

## ✨ Key features

- **The whole route, step by step.** Pick a profession, then From and To, or As far as gold goes with a budget. The route shows by rank, with every trainer visit and every recipe to get as a step of its own.
- **What it all costs.** The card shows the auction house and vendor costs apart, recipes and training, what your crafts are worth, the bottom line and the time. Point at it to see where every figure came from.
- **A gold budget.** Say how much you want to spend and see how far it reaches, the cost per point, and what a little more would reach.
- **It follows along by itself.** As your skill goes up or you learn a recipe, the step you are on moves on and done steps fade.
- **What you need.** A second tab lists every material, what you already have in your bags, bank and mail, and where to get the rest, with Make or Buy for bars, bolts and leathers.
- **Every recipe says how to get it.** A trainer, a vendor, a quest or a world drop, with its price when known.
- **One click to Workbench and your shopping list.** Send to Workbench turns the whole route into one crafting queue; with Workbench 2.8 it also trains your ranks, buys recipe scrolls and learns recipes on your click. Send to shopping list puts everything to buy at the auction house on one list.
- **Follow this route.** With ShortestPathForever or TomTom, every vendor and trainer of the route becomes one journey, nearest vendors first, then the trainers in route order.

## 🎯 Who it's for

- **You want to level a profession** without keeping a guide open beside the game.
- **You want to know the cost first:** how much gold a profession takes to the top, or how far your gold gets you.
- **You use Forever Workbench** and want the whole route queued for you, trainer visits and recipe scrolls included.
- **You levelled a profession before** and want a quick check of what is left, from where you are now.

## 🧭 How it works

1. **Get access and install** with WowUp, as described in [Get it](#-get-it).
2. **Type `/apprentice`** or click the minimap button, and pick a profession.
3. **Choose how far:** From and To, or As far as gold goes with a budget. Read the card.
4. **Send it:** Send to Workbench for the crafts, Send to shopping list for the auction house, and Follow this route for the trainers and vendors. Nothing is bought or crafted until you start it there.

## 🔗 Works with

Apprentice works alone: the whole route, vendor prices, what you hold, and the step you are on. With these it does more:

- **Forever Crafting Ledger.** Auction house prices, and where every recipe is sold or taught.
- **Forever Workbench.** Send to Workbench makes the route one crafting queue; with Workbench 2.8 the queue also trains, buys recipe scrolls and learns. Measured craft times too.
- **Forever Stocking Stuffer.** Send to shopping list puts the auction house part on one list.
- **ShortestPathForever or TomTom.** Follow this route sends every place of the route as one journey, or a waypoint per place.
- **QuestieDB with Questie.** Where the trainers and the vendors stand, for Follow this route.
- **Auctionator.** Auction prices when the Crafting Ledger is not installed.
- **Every TIMID addon.** With two or more installed, their minimap buttons fold into one TIMID button.

## ❓ FAQ

### Where do the routes come from?

From the WoW Forever profession guides on [wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever), checked against the Crafting Ledger's Forever recipe data. The guides say their ranges past 225 are not yet tested on live.

### Does it buy or craft anything?

No. It plans. Nothing is bought or crafted until you start it in Workbench or your shopping list, and every purchase there is your own click.

### Does it need other addons?

No. Everything works with no other addon; the ones above add prices, queues and waypoints.

### Will it slow my game?

It stays closed until you open it, and long work like the journey order is done a little each frame, so the game never stutters.

### Is my data private?

Yes. Your settings stay on your computer. A problem report holds nothing about your character, realm, guild, gold or account, and it leaves the game only when you copy and paste it yourself.

### How do I report a problem?

Click the small red exclamation beside the gear on the Apprentice window, copy the link and paste it into your browser. The form opens already filled in.
