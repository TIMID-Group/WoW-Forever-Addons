## 💡 What it is

Shopping at the auction house for a batch of crafts means searching for each material, comparing prices and buying in the right amounts, one item after another. Forever Stocking Stuffer keeps the list for you. Put items on a list with how many you want and the most you will pay for one, then click **Buy next** at the auction house, once per purchase. It buys the cheapest first and never pays above your max price.

## ✨ Key features

- **Your lists, in the auction house.** A Stocking Stuffer tab at the bottom of the auction house. Each row shows the item, how many you want, how many you have bought so far ("12 of 20"), its rule, its max price, the cheapest listing seen and its status in plain words.
- **One click, one purchase, at exactly the price shown.** The line above Buy next says what the next click buys and what it costs. Stackable goods (cloth, herbs, spices) are bought in one purchase for everything still wanted at or below the max; other items one auction at a time.
- **Never above your max.** Pick **Only at or below** for a fixed max price, or **Any price** for the cheapest first, never more than 20% over the cheapest price in the item's latest search. If the game's price comes back higher, nothing is bought.
- **Counts you can trust.** A purchase counts only once the auction house confirms it, and the counts are kept through a reload or a disconnect. If an answer never comes, the row asks you to check your mail.
- **× and Undo.** Multiply a whole list by a number, say ×3 for three batches, and Undo puts it back.
- **Lists as text.** Export a list to copy it, or Import one. A pasted list comes in switched off, so nothing is bought until you have checked it.
- **Lists from other TIMID addons.** Forever Crafting Ledger's Send to shopping list fills a list for you, and Forever Workbench's Collect from mail step picks up what you bought.

## 🎯 Who it's for

- **You don't have a shopping list addon** and want a plain one: a list, a max price, a button.
- **You craft in batches** and want every material bought in the right amount without searching item by item.
- **You keep an eye on gold** and want a hard limit that is never crossed, whatever the market does.
- **You use the Crafting Ledger** and want its plans turned into a list you can buy from.

## 🧭 How it works

1. **Get access and install** with WowUp, as described in [Get it](#-get-it).
2. **Open the auction house** and click the Stocking Stuffer tab. Press New list.
3. **Add items:** shift-click or drag an item into Add item, or type its number, then how many you want and the most you will pay.
4. **Click Buy next**, once per purchase, until every row says Done. Purchases arrive in your mailbox.

## 🔗 Works with

Stocking Stuffer needs nothing else.

- **Forever Crafting Ledger.** Send to shopping list puts a list's auction house materials here, each with the number still needed and the most to pay for one.
- **Forever Workbench.** Its Collect from mail step picks up what you bought.
- **Auctionator.** The Stocking Stuffer tab sits in the same row as Auctionator's tabs.
- **Every TIMID addon.** With two or more installed, their minimap buttons fold into one TIMID button, and Stocking Stuffer's row says what is left to buy, such as "12 to buy on Raid mats".

## ❓ FAQ

### Does it buy on its own?

No. Every purchase is your click on Buy next. There is no key, no key binding and no command that buys.

### Can it pay more than I meant?

No. Under Only at or below it never pays more for one than the row's max price. Under Any price the max is 20% over the cheapest price in the latest search. If the game's own price comes back higher, the purchase is cancelled and nothing is bought.

### Does it ever sell, post or cancel anything?

No. It only buys what is on your lists, and never your own auctions.

### Will it slow my game?

Away from the auction house it does nothing at all. At the auction house it waits while another TIMID addon is busy there.

### Is my data private?

Yes. Your lists stay on your computer. A problem report holds nothing about your character, realm, guild, gold or account, and it leaves the game only when you copy and paste it yourself.

### How do I report a problem?

Click the small red exclamation beside the gear on the Stocking Stuffer tab, copy the link and paste it into your browser. The form opens already filled in.
